# DIO Public Launch Design

## Objective

Turn the existing DIO humble-brag / engineering site into an investor-grade corporate front door while preserving working product, Vesper and intake infrastructure.

## Audience split

The root site serves investors, strategic partners and enterprise technology leaders. Product pages serve buyers. DIO lore remains visible as platform identity and provenance, but commercial surfaces lead with customer outcomes.

## Public naming law

Sell the outcome. Reveal the lore.

- Exam & Assessment Studio — powered by HOMS
- Performance Evidence Studio — powered by VAMP
- Research Integrity Studio — powered by Sophia
- Evidence Assurance — powered by Evidex
- Programme Assurance — powered by VAMP + META
- Website Launch Studio — powered by Site Studio + Market Command

## Root-site narrative

1. AI workflows are easy to build. Trustworthy AI products are not.
2. DIO is not a collection of AI apps; it is an operating architecture for creating governed AI products.
3. Show commercial product incarnations before deep architecture.
4. Show platform lore as reusable systems and moat.
5. Show controlled engineering proof without claiming market validation.
6. Show the transition from architecture to real market learning.
7. End in two conversion paths: Talk to Vesper or submit a structured intake.

## Vesper

Vesper is the persistent DIO Presence Core persona. The existing Vesper web-chat session/message API remains unchanged. The public skeleton reserves a stable Vesper portrait slot, but no final portrait ships until the post-skeleton media audit establishes the canonical visual identity. The earlier joke/anime-nun concept is explicitly excluded from the public brand. Corporate CTAs open Vesper in broad DIO context; product CTAs pass an incarnation hint.

## Media sequence

Skeleton first. Then one media audit maps every reserved slot to the correct medium: real product artifact, UI capture, diagram, illustration, motion, portrait, video, or no asset. Final assets are created only after the layout and aspect ratios are fixed.

## Intake

The existing governed public intake endpoint remains the submission target. The form adds commercial intent choices including investor / strategic partnership, assessment, performance evidence, research integrity, evidence assurance, programme assurance and website launch.

## Truth boundary

Controlled engineering receipts are not represented as customer validation, revenue proof, investment readiness certification, autonomous authority, or external market success. DIO may state that controlled journeys and product-class mappings exist, while explicitly identifying market validation as the next empirical layer.
