# DIO Public Launch Lock

## Objective

Launch a public-facing DIO portfolio that can attract serious investor / strategic interest while giving customers direct, understandable product entry points.

## In scope

- Investor-grade DIO Workflows root site
- Customer-facing product portfolio
- Vesper conversational entry surface
- Governed public intake
- LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook distribution links
- Media audit after the skeleton
- Google storefront and launch campaign assets after public surfaces are ready

## Out of scope during launch rail

- New DIO core architecture work unless it directly blocks the public launch
- Product-family semantic refactors unrelated to a public claim or route
- Execution-proof expansion for its own sake
- Rebuilding already-proven product pipelines from zero
- Manual bespoke redesign of every product before the first public launch

## Naming law

**Sell the outcome. Reveal the lore.**

Commercial product names lead. HOMS, VAMP, Sophia, Evidex, Vesper, META, Seraph and ARDA remain platform identity, provenance and architectural lore.

## Media law

**Skeleton → media audit → assets → visual QA.**

No joke/anime-nun Vesper asset ships. The canonical Sister Vesper visual identity is deliberately art-directed after the layout is fixed.

## Truth law

Engineering proof is not market validation. DIO does not claim customer demand, repeatable revenue, investment readiness certification or autonomous professional authority until those states are actually evidenced.
