## Media law

1. Real product outputs beat decorative stock imagery.
2. A visual must earn its slot by explaining product, platform, proof or identity.
3. One canonical Vesper identity is derived into all avatar crops; no unrelated variants.
4. Product marketing imagery must lead with customer outcomes; lore can appear as provenance.
5. No asset is final until desktop and mobile crop behaviour are known.
6. YouTube and social assets reuse the same public-offer truth as the website; no separate claim universe.
