RELEASE BOUNDARY

Candidate ≠ approved

Generated images are review candidates. Licensing, source rights, visual QA and external publication authority remain separate gates. No demonstration on this page publishes customer material.

Provenance and validation method

Images are copied from the uploaded original evidence ZIP, decoded with Pillow, SHA-256 checked against the V4 receipt, and listed in assets/evidence/manifest.json. A readable source path is retained for operator review. Where matching source and result are available, the slider can compare them. If evidence is missing, the gallery says so.

File existence and hashes prove that the files were available to the collector, not that the workflow's output quality, authorship rights or market release was independently validated.

04 / An important distinction

Beautiful isn't the same as true.

Pictora's generated scenes are creative material. A reconstructed photograph or inpainted document must never silently be represented as unaltered evidence.
